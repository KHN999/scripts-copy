This is a crime story for its first half. Play it that way.

1. Ma Shwe Zin looks identical alive and dead. Same face, same costume, same make-up, same warm skin, same weight. Nothing pale, nothing see-through, nothing decayed, no wound — not even in shot 46 where the knife goes through her. If a frame reads as "ghost", it is wrong. The only supernatural facts on screen in the whole film are three: an orchestra that plays with nobody touching it (34), a blade that passes through her (46), and a bulb that bursts (48).

2. She has two references and they are ONE woman. In costume for shots 1, 4, 21–25, 32–50; in a plain blouse with her hair pinned for 5, 6, 9, 11–16 and the whole ending, 56–62. Build the plain-clothes reference from the costumed one so the face carries. If they come out looking like two different actresses, rebuild — the ending only works if the audience sees the same person without the costume.

3. U Ba Myint is the monster. He is a living man who poisons a woman and hides two bodies. His reference is deliberately respectable — someone villagers defer to — and the first crack is shot 8, alone in a tent with a cash box. Keep the menace out of his face until 44, where he walks onto a lit stage holding a knife.

4. No gore anywhere. Shot 22 is a trace of blood at the corner of her lip and nothing more. Shot 46 has no blood at all. The pit in 41 holds sacks, ledgers and dust — no remains. The bodies in 55 are a mound of earth seen from a distance.

5. Backstage is the main location. Eighteen shots live behind the stage: dressing tents, hurricane lanterns, costume trunks, rolled backdrops. Keep it warm, cluttered and lived-in. The stage itself gets the saturated colour — printed curtains in hot pink and gold, bulbs on twisted wire.

6. Get the hsaing waing right. The pat waing is twenty-one tuned drums slung inside a tall circular gilded frame with mirror-glass inlay, the player seated inside it — not a drum kit. Beside it the kyi waing gong circle, a rack of maung gongs, the hne oboe, si and wa. It matters most in 31, 34 and 64.

Nothing readable, ever. The ledgers (41), the identity cards (43) and her letter (53) are all shot angled away, out of focus or face down. If letterforms resolve, throw the image away.

Three shots carry no reference — 3 (the convoy on the road), 28 (the funeral) and 55 (the burial ground). All three are deliberately anonymous; generate them free-standing.
